This example should be a decent test of the search algorithm as both subunits have the lowest required symmetry.

Running C3 and C2 should give a pretty much perfect solution for edge-capped cube. Running inverted C3 and C2 should give a poorer fit for edge-capped cube and may find another shape class is better. Will be interesting to see...

For C3
 - Take the plane of the dummy atoms to get the plane vector
 - Align the plane vector with the centroid of the dummies (or centroid of the molecule)

For C2
 - Take the plane of all atoms to get the plane vector
 - Find the vector between the dummy atoms
 - Take the cross product of (dummy atom vector) and (plane vector)
 - Align the cross product output with the centroid (molecule or dummies)
